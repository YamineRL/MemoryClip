## What's new

- **Settings → Shortcuts opens instead of crashing.** A resource bundle
  the app has always needed was never copied into the .app — this was a
  latent crash in every release, and it is finally gone.
- **The missing-language callout can be dismissed.** The "already read a
  screenshot in X" banner now offers Dismiss: the listed languages will
  not re-announce themselves on every screenshot, unless you tick one —
  that is you saying you want it after all.
- **Privacy hardening from an app-wide audit.** Secret clips are now
  invisible to Shortcuts and Spotlight answers (existence and source app
  included — they never carried plaintext). Silent store writes from the
  panel now log instead of vanishing, a drag-time race is closed, and
  secret persistence failures report instead of passing in silence.
