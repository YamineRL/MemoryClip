## What's new

- **Search understands a sentence.** Type "that error about the deploy failing"
  and it finds the clip that says *deployment to production failed*. Every word
  has to match rather than the whole phrase being looked for verbatim, and words
  are reduced to the stem they share with their dictionary form. Search now also
  covers what the on-device model wrote about a clip — its summary, title and
  tags — and both translations.
- **Drag a clip out of the panel** into any app. A screenshot travels as the file
  it already is, never as a copy of its bytes.
- **Select several clips at once.** ⇧-click and ⇧↑/↓ extend, ⌘-click picks clips
  out one at a time, and Return pastes the lot in order. Vim mode gains `v`.
- **Import your history back.** **Settings → History → Import…** reads a JSON
  export, keeping the times clips were first copied and skipping any already
  there, so moving to a new Mac keeps the history.
- **Never capture from apps you name.** **Settings → Privacy → Excluded apps**.
  Nothing copied there reaches the store at all, and unlike the built-in
  password-manager list it keeps applying with sensitive-content filtering off.
- **Always paste as plain text into apps you name.** **Settings → General →
  Pasting**, seeded with the usual terminals and editors, every entry removable.
- **Fourteen more transforms**, and the menu is grouped: Case, JSON, Encoding,
  Color, Time and Lines. Colours convert between hex, `rgb()` and `hsl()`, and a
  Unix timestamp becomes a date and back.
- **Notes translate into the language you choose**, not always English —
  **Settings → Translation → Language**, which now governs notes and the panel's
  preview alike. Existing installs keep English until a language is picked.
