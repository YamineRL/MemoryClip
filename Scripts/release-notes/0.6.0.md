## What's new

- **Copied links are stripped of tracking parameters.** `?igsh=`, `utm_*`,
  `fbclid`, `si`, Amazon `ref_`, and per-site rules for Instagram, YouTube, X,
  TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Spotify, Reddit, eBay and AliExpress. Paste is
  clean everywhere, not just from the panel.
- **⌘C copies your selection in the preview pane.** Select a few words and copy
  them, instead of right-clicking for the whole clip.
- **`Use original` gives the untouched URL back** from any cleaned clip, and a
  `Cleaned` badge marks them.
- **New `Links` settings** under Privacy: turn cleaning off, keep `utm_*`, or
  exclude specific sites. Links carrying a token, code or signature are never
  touched.
- **`Clean Link` transform** for links captured before this release.
- The footer counts every match in the store, not just the loaded page.
- The hint bubble is readable over any card, in both appearances.
