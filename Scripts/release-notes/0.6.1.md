## What's new

- **⌘Y opens the preview while searching.** Type a query, pick a card with
  ↑ ↓, press ⌘Y. Space still types into the search field.
- **The panel reopens on the newest clip**, not wherever you last scrolled.
- **More tracking parameters removed**: Instagram `stkn`, Threads, Twitch,
  Snapchat, Etsy, Walmart, IMDb, Netflix and Google, plus extra TikTok,
  Facebook, LinkedIn, Amazon, Spotify and Reddit share tags.
