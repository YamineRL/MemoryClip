## What's new

- **Passwords and API keys are encrypted with the Secure Enclave.** Copied
  private keys, tokens (AWS, GitHub, OpenAI, Anthropic, Slack, Stripe), JWTs,
  connection strings and one-time codes show as a label and a mask. Touch ID
  reveals one for 30 seconds, and the clipboard clears 90 seconds after a paste.
  Settings › Privacy can drop them or keep them as plain text instead.
- **Pinboards.** Group pinned clips into named, ordered boards shown as chips
  under the search bar. ⌘P files a clip, ⌃1-9 jumps to a board, ⌥⇧← → reorders
  within it. Your existing pins are in `Pinned`.
- **Search operators**: `app:`, `type:`, `is:pinned`, `is:secret`, `in:<board>`,
  `after:7d`, `before:`, `on:`. Prefix `-` to exclude. Type `?` for the list.
- **Edit a clip before pasting it.** ⌘I opens the editor, ⌘S saves,
  ⌘Return saves and pastes, ⌘Z restores the original.
- **Shortcuts and Spotlight**: Get Latest Clip, Search Clips, Copy Clip and
  Open MemoryClip.
- **History keeps 5,000 clips for 90 days by default.** Pick a limit from 200
  to Unlimited, or keep clips for a year. Settings shows the disk space used,
  and lowering a limit says how many clips it will delete first. Limits you set
  yourself are kept.
- The welcome tour lets you record your own panel shortcut if another app
  already uses ⇧⌘V.
- The OCR switch lives in one place, under Settings › Panel.
