## What's new

- **The original text stays in the preview while it's translated.** The text
  under a screenshot has two tabs, Original and the translation, and ⌘T
  switches between them.
- The preview no longer jumps while a translation streams in.
- Tables show as grids in the translation too.
- An article beside a sidebar is no longer read as a two-column table.
- The Quick Look hint sits at the top of the preview, off the text.
